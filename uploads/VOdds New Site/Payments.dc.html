FAQ

Is there a minimum deposit?
Minimums vary slightly by method; the amount is always shown before you confirm a deposit.
How long do withdrawals take?
Crypto and e-wallet withdrawals are typically processed within hours; card and bank wire withdrawals can take a few business days.
Do I need a separate wallet per bookmaker?
No. One VOdds balance funds bets across every connected sportsbook.

SEO Block

VOdds lets bettors fund a single balance and use it across every connected sportsbook and exchange, instead of managing separate wallets for each provider. Deposits are accepted by card, bank wire, and cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USDC and TRON, alongside e-wallets such as Skrill, Neteller, Payz and Jetonbank. Card and e-wallet deposits are typically instant, crypto credits within minutes, and bank wire within a few business days. Withdrawals follow the same method wherever possible, with crypto and e-wallet payouts processed fastest.
