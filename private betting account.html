A Private Betting Account is a direct account opened with a named partner sportsbook — PS3838, SingBet, OrbitX or another connected provider — and managed under a bettor's VOdds wallet. These accounts are built for bettors who need higher limits or sharper Asian Handicap pricing than a standard connected book offers, without the paperwork of opening and funding a separate sportsbook account directly. VOdds handles setup, funding and ongoing support, while the bettor keeps one login and one balance across every provider.
