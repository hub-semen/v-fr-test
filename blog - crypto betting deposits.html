Getting Started with Crypto Deposits: BTC, USDT and More

Jul 28, 2026 · 4 min read · By the VOdds Team

Crypto is the fastest way to fund a VOdds balance. There's no card processing delay and no bank cut-off time — a confirmed transaction credits your wallet and you're ready to bet.

Supported coins

  • Bitcoin (BTC) — the original network; deposits typically need one to three confirmations.
  • Tether (USDT) — a dollar-pegged stablecoin, available on multiple networks; check the network before sending.
  • Ethereum (ETH) — confirms faster than Bitcoin on most days, subject to network gas conditions.
  • USD Coin (USDC) — a second stablecoin option, pegged 1:1 to the dollar.
  • TRON (TRX) — low fees and fast confirmation times, popular for smaller deposits.

Making your first deposit

Open the deposit screen in your VOdds wallet, pick your coin, and you'll get a unique deposit address (and, for USDT, a network to confirm — ERC-20, TRC-20 or similar). Send from your exchange or personal wallet to that address. Sending the wrong coin to the wrong network address is the most common way deposits go missing, so double-check both before confirming.

Confirmation times

Your balance updates once the transaction reaches the required number of network confirmations, not the moment it's broadcast. This is typically a few minutes for TRX and USDT-TRC20, and can run longer for BTC during busy network periods. The deposit screen shows the live confirmation count so you can track progress.

Why it matters for betting

A funded balance means you can act on a price the moment the Odds Scanner shows it, rather than waiting on a bank transfer to clear first. For bettors moving in and out of positions regularly, that speed is often the deciding factor in choosing crypto over traditional payment methods.
